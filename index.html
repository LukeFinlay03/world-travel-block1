Safety in one minute

    Want in?

    The honest version.

    • What it is: about 16 weeks of real hiking and landscapes. Not luxury. Tents, huts, yurts and hostels.
    • What it costs: around $15,000 each, and about three quarters of that comes from real quotes.
    • What it asks: saving the money, getting fit, and living with the same people for four months.
    • What it doesn't ask: to be an expert, or to promise the later blocks. You say yes to one block at a time.
    • If it's a no: no hard feelings, truly. A reluctant yes is worse for everyone.

    Interested? Talk to whoever sent you this link. A good first step is a weekend overnight hike to see if you like it.
